# Question shapes

Adapt these to the specific record. Name the meeting, PR, person or date.

## Impact

- What would have happened if you hadn't done this?
- Who is better off because of it, and how would they say so?
- What does this unblock, or what can someone do now that they couldn't before?
- Is there a number: time saved, alerts cut, a launch date pulled in, an incident shortened?

## Evidence

- What can someone click on to see this: a PR, a doc, a Slack thread, a dashboard?
- Did anyone say anything about it? That's feedback worth recording.

## Scope and influence

- Who else was involved, and what was your part versus theirs?
- Did this cross a team or collective boundary?
- Did you change someone's mind, or the plan? What was it before?

## By record type

- **Meeting with notes:** What was decided? What did you push for, and did it land? What happens next, and who owns it?
- **Calendar event with no notes:** You had "X" with Y. Did anything come out of it?
- **1:1:** Anything about your growth, or theirs? Any feedback either way?
- **PR merged:** Why this change, why now? Anything subtle in the design? Did it reach production, and did it work?
- **PR still open:** What's holding it up? Is that on you?
- **Interview:** Only the count matters: how many, and the hire/no-hire split. Don't ask about content.
- **Completed todos:** Which of these took more than it looks like?
- **Overdue todos:** Still worth doing? What got in the way?
- **Last week's open threads:** You said X was blocked on Y. Where did it land?

## Blind spots

The store can't see these, so always ask:

- Roughly how many PRs did you review? (Only the number matters.)
- Any Slack threads where you answered a question, made a call, or unblocked someone?
- Any docs, RFCs or proposals written or commented on?
- Any incidents or on-call?
- Did you help anyone, or did someone help you?

## Wrapping up

- Anything else? Anything you're quietly proud of?
- Anything that went badly, or that you'd do differently?
- What's the one thing from this week your manager should know?
